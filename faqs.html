FAQs

Answers to common questions.

Do I need any experience?

Not at all. Our workshops are designed for all levels, including complete beginners.

What should I bring?

Just yourself! We provide aprons, ingredients and equipment.

Do I get to take my bakes home?

Yes, you'll go home with everything you make.

Can you cater for dietary requirements?

Let us know when booking and we'll do our best to help.
