FAQ

Everything You Want To Know

Can’t find what you’re looking for? Get in touch and we’ll be happy to help.

Do I need any baking experience?

Not at all. Our workshops are designed for every level, including complete beginners. Our bakers will guide you through every step.

What should I bring to a workshop?

Just yourself! We provide aprons, ingredients, equipment and containers to take your bakes home.

Can you cater for dietary requirements?

Let us know when you book and we'll do our best to help. Some classes can be adapted for vegetarian or dairy-free diets.

How long are gift vouchers valid for?

Our gift vouchers are valid for 12 months from the date of purchase and can be used for any workshop.

Do you take custom cake orders?

Yes. Get in touch at least a week before your event and we'll talk you through flavours, sizes and designs.

Can I cancel or change my booking?

You can move your booking to another date up to 72 hours before your class. See our terms for full details.
